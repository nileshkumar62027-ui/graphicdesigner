// The brief

## From a logo to a full brand kit

KlairKlint is a young T-shirt start-up built for students at NIT Rourkela. The logo and name were already in place. I joined as a designer and my job was to turn them into a complete kit, so every item a customer touches feels like it comes from the same brand.

I designed the tags, boxes, bag, cards and thank-you note, and kept them on one set of rules: dark charcoal, a white mono-line logo, one orange accent and a slanted dash frame.

// Brand rules

## The rules that hold the kit together

### Logo

KlairKlint logo lockup on charcoal

The paperclip-K mark with the thin mono-line KLAIRKLINT wordmark. White on dark, charcoal on light. Keep clear space equal to the height of the K. Never stretch, recolour or add shadows.

### Colour

**Charcoal**\
#1D1D1E\
Main background

**White**\
#FFFFFF\
Logo and text

**Sunset Orange**\
#F3733D\
Accent, used sparingly

**Slate Blue**\
#4D5B8A\
Brand card shapes

**Plum**\
#755B8D\
Mark badge

Usage guide: about 70% charcoal, 20% white, with orange, slate blue and plum as small accents. Never set body text on orange.

### Typography

Montserrat BoldMontserrat LightThank You.

Montserrat in two weights for the tagline and text, the custom thin wordmark for the name only, and an elegant script reserved for thank-you moments.

### Tagline lockup

**Redefining***Young***Fashion**

Always stacked in three lines: bold, light between two thin rules, bold. On dark layouts the word Fashion may take the orange accent.

### Signature motif

A slanted dash frame runs around the lid of every box. It is the one decorative element in the kit, so it is never used inside the logo or on text.

### Voice

“You just made our day. Hope we made yours too!”

Warm, short and friendly, the way one student would talk to another. No hard-sell language.

#### Do

- Use the white logo on charcoal
- Keep the tagline lockup together
- Use one orange accent per layout
- Leave generous empty space

#### Don't

- Stretch, rotate or recolour the logo
- Place the logo on busy photos
- Mix in other fonts
- Use orange for large text blocks

## Every touchpoint, one look

KlairKlint hang tags next to a black box lid

### Hang tags and box lid

Die-cut charcoal tags with the mark and tagline on one face and the wordmark and website on the other, shown with the dash-framed lid.

KlairKlint gift box

### Gift box

A rigid box that opens to a folded tee and extras, with the slanted dash frame and the tagline lockup on the lid.

KlairKlint shipping box

### Shipping box

A corrugated mailer in black with the dash frame on top, the wordmark on the side and the website on the edge.

KlairKlint shopping bag

### Shopping bag

A matte black carry bag with only the mark and tagline, centred and small, so the bag stays quiet.

KlairKlint business cards

### Business cards

Rounded-corner cards with a curved dark panel for contact details on one side and a QR code with the tagline on the other.

KlairKlint brand card

### Brand card

Slate blue and orange ribbons meet at a plum badge holding the mark.

KlairKlint thank-you card

### Thank-you card

Packed with every order, with an orange full stop as the only accent.
